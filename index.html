What the Numbers Mean

PM2.5

Tiny particles from smoke and exhaust that can reach deep into your lungs.

PM10

Larger particles like dust and pollen that irritate your nose and throat.

O3

Ground-level ozone, which builds up on hot, sunny days and makes breathing harder.

NO2

A gas from cars and power plants, usually highest near busy roads.
